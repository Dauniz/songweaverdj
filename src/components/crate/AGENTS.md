## Radio playback rules
- Spotify holds `[current, skip]`; both doors' own skips are pre-planned (landing plan, `finishSkip`) and sent as a pair on finish/landing; Spotify-started songs are re-sent once with their skip door. Why: Spotify never runs dry or plays the wrong door.
- Backgrounded tabs (iOS pauses them): on return, away-time is frozen (never counts toward the 90 s pause rule) and Crate re-syncs with Spotify's real state, adopting a foreign song fresh; a screen wake lock is held while a session is live. Why: sessions must survive leaving the preview on phones.
- Songweaver "Next" button uses skipNow: plain Spotify next only when Crate's skip door is lined up behind the current song, else plays the door directly as a skip. Why: your own Spotify queue must never decide the skip.
- Spotify pushes take the fast path: no position lookup first (aim from the last poll plus measured latency), and the browser sends the live device ID. When a lined-up song starts, Crate checks Spotify's "next up" and pushes its skip door again only if it's missing. Why: a short push keeps the re-buffer glitch small, and a skip door must always be next.
- Skip ladder (advance): 1 skip = same road (era hops 1–3 yrs), 2–3 = other road, 4+ = New Angle. Artist cooling = skipped in last 5 songs (demoted); block at 5 session skips; heard songs excluded for 50. Doors are validated ahead against the state they open into (doorProblem in checkPlan); an opened door is never swapped. Why: no mid-song pushes from rule changes.
- Chat steering, not chips: mid-session chat messages carry a "(Session is live — steer instead of restarting)" marker; the AI answers with steer_only + steer_note (+ optional picks) and radio-context's steerSession re-scouts B, v and C's w under the steer note while C stays queued exactly as sent to Spotify (replacing a queued door would glitch audio). A steerNote persists for the rest of the session, is passed to nextPathTrack, and forces the Era instant path through the AI. Steering ends a running prompt playlist; it never writes one. Why: the listener steers in their own words without pausing the music, and C must never be swapped mid-play.
- Prompt playlist: a prompt's 6 picks (from the chosen card onward) are fixed finish doors in order (promptQueue); the AI's start_road picks the start road; any skip/reroot or reaching song 6 returns to the normal maze. promptQueue is prompt-start-only — chat steering never uses or extends it. Why: the user asked for those songs.
- Alternative roads (Wormhole, Texture, Scene, Deep cuts; src/lib/alt-roads.ts) replace Vibe/Era while on (advance() suspends the default ladder, labels show the road name, reserves use the road shortlist): code narrows the library to ~15 songs, then the AI picks one. Ladders come from the trailing skips in the history: Texture/Deep shift texture/era every 2 skips, Scene 1=origin, 2–3=featured, 4+=new angle, Wormhole has none. Why: cheap, road-true picks with no new client state.
- Memory tiers: anchors > history profile > Feedbacker notes > steer insights (origin `steer`, saved per steer, deduped per session) > session observations. Cross-session synthesis may promote notes/steers/observations only with 3+ sessions of support plus agreeing listening; library sync creates no memories. Why: stated words are hints, behaviour is evidence.
- Session start detects an already-open Spotify app via the device list (findOpenSpotify), not the paused-song state: Start button keeps a playing song (adopt + usual skip-door push) or resumes an open paused app; prompt/search/welcome ignore what Spotify played and send [first, skip door] together; the Open Spotify popup appears only when no device is open. Same on every device. Why: /me/player reports the last song even after the app closes, and the popup must never send users to an app that's already open.
- Poll answers that left before a song change are discarded, and after a finish hand-over the just-finished song reported by Spotify (until the finish song shows, max ~5 s) is never a skip or manual pick; the finish song counts as seen only once Spotify reports it. Why: Spotify lags ~1 s after a play command, and reading that lag as two skips reset the whole maze.
- A live session with no followed song keeps polling Spotify (wait loop) until a song appears; it ends only after 90 s with no open device. The Start button adopts a loaded-but-paused song 5 s after resuming an open app; the dot shows a grey "waiting" state until Crate hears music or follows a song. Why: a session must never sit green with nothing watching.
- Mid-session search and prompt picks never replace the playing song: the pick becomes the finish door B (prompt: its picks queue behind the current song), v is re-scouted from B's direction, C and its planned w stay as queued. Nothing is pushed until the current song finishes. Why: tailoring must not cut off the music or glitch Spotify.
- Hand-over lock: a steer/search/prompt arriving while handOver is in flight (swapping) is held as pendingSteer and applied ~1 s after the finish song is handed over; dropped if you moved on. Why: changing B/v mid-hand-over made Spotify play the old B and Crate read it as a skip.
- Session start (search/prompt/welcome/resume): foreign Spotify reports are ignored until Spotify reports the sent song, max 8 s after the send. Why: Spotify's lag made Crate adopt the previously playing song and rebuild the maze.
- Fresh session start order: pick C → send [A, C] (A alone after 6 s, C pushed when ready) → w (planned on send) → B avoiding C → v. Why: A must start fast with its skip door already queued.
- A song you start in Spotify mid-song (not one Crate queued) counts as a skip: it takes C's place, C's landing plan is retargeted to it so [your song, w] is pushed at once, and a running prompt playlist ends. A judgeForeignPick AI check (skipped when the artist matches recent maze songs) turns a clearly different pick into a steer note that re-scouts B and v. Why: no maze reset and no wait-then-glitch on your own picks.
